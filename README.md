A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a mother rocks the cradle while the tide comes in and goes out outside, as it has since the moon first pulled the sea.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (224 characters, UTF-8).

## The text

> Your mother rocks the cradle. Back. Forth. Back. Forth. Outside, the tide comes in, and goes out, and comes in, as it has since the moon first pulled the sea. Moonlight lies on the floor. The cradle rocks. The tide comes in.
