A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a mother rocks the cradle while the tide comes in outside, moonlight on the floor, and the moon climbs higher.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (248 characters, UTF-8).

## The text

> Your mother rocks the cradle. Back. Forth. Back. Forth. Outside, the tide comes in, and goes out, and comes in, as it has for longer than the house has stood. Moonlight lies on the floor. The cradle rocks. The tide comes in. The moon climbs higher.
