A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a drop of water in a cave, falling once a minute for ten thousand years, building stone grain by grain until the floor rises to meet the ceiling.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (262 characters, UTF-8).

## The text

> In the cave, a drop of water falls from the ceiling once a minute. It has fallen for ten thousand years. Each drop leaves a little more stone on the floor below, grain by grain, until the floor rises to meet the ceiling. The cave is patient. It is still growing.
