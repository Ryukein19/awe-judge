A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — you stand on a cliff at dusk and watch a whale surface below, breathe once, slow, and go on, having crossed this sea a hundred times and bound to cross it a hundred more.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (257 characters, UTF-8).

## The text

> You stand on the cliff at dusk. Below you, the sea is dark. A whale surfaces. It breathes, once, slow. The spout rises, and falls. It has crossed this sea a hundred times, and it will cross it a hundred more. You are still standing there. The whale goes on.
