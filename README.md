A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a grandmother holds your grandchild's hand, a chain of hands reaching back through generations to hands that held a plough, a net, a child, before there was a road here.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` (276 characters, UTF-8).

## The text

> Your grandmother holds your hand. Warm. Rough. Small. It held your mother's hand, and her mother's hand before that, and her mother's hand before that, all the way back to hands that held a plough, a net, a child, before there was a road here. Her hand is still holding yours.
